# Ridge CRM planning

Before discussing or changing anything about Discovery, Catch-up, Refresh,
or the outreach engines generally, read the relevant section of
`docs/outreach-engine-plan.md` fresh — don't rely on chat memory, a prior
session's summary, or any internal planning scratch file. That doc is the
single source of truth for this project's design; when it disagrees with
anything else, the doc wins. When a new decision gets made, update the doc
in the same sitting, per its own "Keeping this in sync" section at the
bottom — don't let a real decision live only in chat.

Same rule for invoicing, commission payment tracking, or anything else
money-in/operations-shaped (as opposed to outreach/revenue-reporting):
read `docs/operations-accounting-plan.md` fresh first, it's the source of
truth for that scope, and update it in the same sitting a new decision
gets made, per its own "Keeping this in sync" section.
